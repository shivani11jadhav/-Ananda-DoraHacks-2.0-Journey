## 📖 The Story Behind Ananda

Ananda was built as part of DoraHacks 2.0, a 10-day global hackathon experience conducted through the GirlsWhoYap Fellowship.

The challenge was not only to build a working product but also to launch it publicly, attract users, gather feedback, and continuously improve the experience.

Our team created Ananda, a digital well-being platform designed around calm, connection, creativity, and meaningful digital experiences.

Instead of feeling like a traditional wellness application, Ananda was designed as a small interactive digital village where users could explore different spaces for support, reflection, companionship, and community.

The project was launched publicly, reached 50+ users, collected valuable feedback, ranked among the Top 100 Products on Product Hunt, and was recognized among the Top 50 Products at DoraHacks 2.0.

## 🌿 About Ananda
## 🌿 About Ananda

Ananda is a digital well-being platform designed to make emotional support feel more human, approachable, and comforting.

Instead of placing everything inside a single overwhelming interface, Ananda creates a small interactive world where users can explore different spaces based on what they need in that moment — guidance, companionship, creativity, or connection.

The goal was simple:

> Guide me. Listen to me. Give me something to do. Help me connect.

Built during DoraHacks 2.0, Ananda focused on creating a calm digital experience that encourages self-reflection, emotional well-being, and meaningful interactions.

---
## 🏡 The Four Spaces of Ananda

### 🏮 GuideBot — The Lantern Cottage

A supportive AI companion designed to help users navigate difficult emotions through grounding exercises, reflection, and practical coping techniques.

**Features**
- Grounding exercises
- Reflection prompts
- Coping toolkits
- Emotional support guidance

---

### 🫂 FriendBot — The Cozy Porch

A conversational companion focused on listening rather than advising.

**Features**
- Open conversations
- Daily check-ins
- Judgment-free interaction
- Emotional companionship

---

### 🎨 ActivityZone — The Creative Workshop

A collection of activities designed to help users pause, reflect, and recharge.

**Features**
- Journaling
- Creative writing
- Mindfulness activities
- Reading resources
- Yoga and light movement

---

### 🏮 Connect — The Community Space

A space where users can anonymously share experiences and connect through supportive interactions.

**Features**
- Community conversations
- Share your day
- Supportive replies
- Human connection
